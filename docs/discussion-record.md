## Corrections and qualifications adopted in this record

1. Earlier citations to Matritenses RP_264v did not establish the claimed Mixcoatl–Chimalma and Itzpapalotl passages. The relevant checked modern commentary was found at Telleriano 385_04v. Underlying older sources still require direct inspection.
2. The story's first-fire statement does not by itself verify a rotating Cosmic Fire Drill or a literal celestial-axis tilt.
3. Xolotl's parentage, Chimalma–Xochiquetzal equivalence, and Mixcoatl's Toci/Coatlicue descent remain unverified here.
4. “Father-like” is a participant reading, not a verified Nahuatl title or etymology.
5. Ometeotl as a mandatory unified root remains a question rather than a settled premise.
6. Divine Quetzalcoatl and Ce Acatl/Topiltzin's narrative identity are distinguishable records even when investigating their overlap.
7. A cycle that begins and continues forever differs from one eternal in both temporal directions. An origin cannot be deduced from recurrence alone.
8. The proposal that all variants can be reconciled cyclically is a hypothesis. Variation can also reflect incompatible accounts, historical change, political adaptation, or incomplete preservation.
9. Modern physical analogies do not establish ancient knowledge of galactic dynamics.
10. The promised larger catalogue was not delivered in the supplied exchange. This repository records it as future work.
