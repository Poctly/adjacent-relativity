## Inspected references

### S01 — UNAM, Gran Diccionario Náhuatl: mixcoatl

URL: https://gdn.iib.unam.mx/diccionario/mixcoatl

Type: institutional lexical compilation, including Wimmer and references to earlier works; not a single primary narrative.

Limited checked support: hunting associations especially among Otomi and Chichimec peoples; links or doubling with Tezcatlipoca and Camaxtli; a Topiltzin father relation. The entry also discusses stellar associations of the plural name. These do not establish every proposed universal identity or parentage.

### S02 — UNAM Tlachia, Telleriano, folio 385_04v

URL: https://tlachia.iib.unam.mx/telleriano/385_04v

Type: manuscript presentation with **modern explanatory commentary**, transcription, and references to older narratives. Separate a manuscript gloss from the editor's cross-source synthesis.

Limited checked support in the commentary: Mixcoatl/Camaxtli and Red associations; Tezcatlipoca's name change and first fire; variant warrior groups; Itzpapalotl's white-flint episode; Mixcoatl–Chimalman parentage and Quilaztli's care of Ce Acatl. The commentary cites HMP and the Codex Chimalpopoca material.

Next step: inspect the cited older editions and passages directly. The checked fire passage reports fire-making after restored heavens; it does **not establish the proposed axial-tilt mechanism**. Distinguish source wording about flints from interpretation involving a fire drill.

## Citation standard for new entries

Supply author/editor, work, edition, language, chapter or folio, page, stable URL where available, and a short supporting excerpt or careful paraphrase. State whether support comes from a primary narrative, manuscript gloss, translation, modern commentary, or inference. Record publication/access dates separately. If a source does not support the earlier claim, preserve that correction.
