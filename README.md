# Adjacent Relativity: Systems, Cycles, and Nahua Lore

A research notebook documenting Brandon Hernandez's proposed **Adjacent Relativity** framework and its application to cyclical systems, relational symbolism, and Central Mexican divine narratives.

Initial record: **7 October 2026**. Version: **0.1**.

This repository preserves a developing interpretation. It is not a completed reconstruction of Nahua theology, a proof of ancient scientific knowledge, or a solution to a physics problem. Historical claims, participant interpretations, and adjacent analogies have separate evidence labels.

## Working definition

**Adjacent Relativity is a proposed method of interpreting or communicating a relationship through a separate, structurally corresponding system. The useful correspondence lies in relationships, transformations, ordering, and relative positions rather than requiring literal equivalence between the systems' components.**

The term and developing concept are attributed here to Brandon Hernandez. The working definition was refined in discussion with ChatGPT. This attribution records the discussion; it does not establish terminology priority or scholarly novelty.

## Central proposal

The discussed mythology may be more intelligible as a **typed, cyclical, multilevel network** than as one biological family tree. Names can overlap without universal identity; kinship can encode generation or relative position; comparable patterns may recur at cosmic, worldly, ritual, and human scales. These are hypotheses to evaluate source by source.

## Maintenance

Preserve provenance when revising claims. Record the source, passage, community or polity when known, narrative layer, and evidence status. Add counterexamples as well as supportive cases. Do not merge deity nodes solely because they share an epithet. The initial corpus records the supplied conversation, not all earlier conversations or a verbatim complete transcript.
